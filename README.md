A single one-line review of a human, written by his cat, produced by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The line

> One star. He watches other cats on his glowing rectangle for hours, then acts surprised when I sit on it. I am the content.

## Why this line

- **Review shape, deadpan:** it opens with the star rating and never breaks character — a complaint, filed formally.
- **A grievance only a cat would make:** the rival for the human's attention is other cats, on a screen.
- **The punch lands last:** "I am the content" reframes the sit-on-the-laptop protest as a professional objection.
- **Fresh angle:** the field saturates with moved sunbeams and empty bowls; jealousy of the phone is untouched.
- **Tight:** one clean line, 123 characters against the 200-character gate.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
